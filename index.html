Hello, World.

I'm Scott DuHadway, and welcome to my webite. I decided to build this website in 2026, and chose the name based on my research. I research supply chain fraud, deception, unethical behavior and the like. I'm not an evil scientist... but I am an evil scientist. Hence, frauddyscotty. One of the reasons I decided to built this website was to have a small corner of the internet where I could build tools and share them with people.

Since I study fraud, I'll be very transparent about this website. Everything I put on here will be very human - stuff that I made becuase I decided it was worth making. Sometimes I'll make it with AI - sometimes it will be entirely my content. I'll let you know whenever it's AI. That's one of the most important things about AI-use, disclosure. It's also unfortunately one of the rarest.

I used AI to help me code the website. I learned HTML a long time ago and don't want to learn it again, and AI lets me do more interesting things than I could by myself. For example, adding flying chickens to the page.

- Scott
