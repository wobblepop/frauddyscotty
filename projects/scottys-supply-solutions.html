Tier 1: Scotty's Supply Solutions (cleanroom assembly — student-proposed model)
CPU packaging
Die on substrate + IHS (Auras, nickel-plated copper) + TIM (Indium Corp). $589 OEM tray price. 80% cost = silicon die.
RAM assembly
8x DRAM ICs on PCB + passives + heat spreader + SPD EEPROM. Legacy ~$25/module, current spot ~$75-$125 (~3-5x increase). [Student doc claimed 18x; actual increases per Counterpoint/TrendForce are ~3-5x cumulative by early 2026]
SSD assembly
2-4 NAND ICs + controller IC + DRAM cache on M.2 PCB. Legacy ~$40, current spot ~$80-$160 (~2-4x increase). Firmware + testing. [Student doc claimed 8x; actual NAND increases are ~2-4x per TrendForce data]

A summary of the primary obstacles each supply chain must address.

Optical mouse Execution on cost; undercut branded alternatives No electronics sourcing experience. Cost model built from scratch. Supplier information asymmetry (they know costs, we don't). 35-50 day component lead times from Asia. Build cost model for PCB assembly + injection molding. Establish relationships with Pan Taiwan, Lectronix, LECC, GQEM. Secure sea freight pricing.
Ground coffee Loss leader for brand; minimize loss while maintaining quality Negative margin must stay controlled. Quality failure damages brand. Colombia vs. Brazil cost/quality tradeoff. Brazil requires 6-month partnership development + importer agent. Finalize contract with Joe's Garage Coffee. Evaluate green bean vs. finished coffee from Colombia distributor. Begin Brazil direct trade farmer outreach (Amazonas/Minas Gerais).
AI t-shirt Collectible weekly drops; stability + agility Production suspended: Section 301 tariffs on China processing make original 4-country chain unviable (tariffs + logistics > retail price). 85-115 day lead time incompatible with weekly drops. Inventory scattered across 4 countries. Evaluate USMCA nearshore route (Mexico/Canada, 0% tariff, 5-day shipping) vs. optimized Asian route. Establish GOEX Apparel as primary nearshore supplier. Separate blank sourcing (MTS) from print operations (MTO).
California roll Feed employees + public; preserve cultural brand asset No demand forecasting (shortages + overproduction). Inconsistent ingredient quality (avocado, crab). Scattered budgets across departments. Food safety gaps between kitchen and outside vendor. $2-$4 variable cost per roll with no centralized tracking. Map end-to-end costs and establish centralized budget. Implement demand forecasting for ~1,000 rolls/day. Standardize food safety across internal and vendor operations. Lock in contracts with TransOcean, Mission Produce, JFC International.
Commuter bicycle Flagship lifestyle brand; healthy profit margin Previous manager retired with zero documentation and was sole Taiwanese-language contact. Supplier oversaturation in Taiwan makes selection difficult. 32 components across 3 supplier tiers to coordinate. Language barrier with all Taiwan-based suppliers. Attend Taipei International Cycle Show to evaluate suppliers. Establish APM Co. (Taiwan) as primary and Shimano (Japan) as drivetrain specialist. Set up 3PL model ($1,540/mo vs. $21,940 traditional [student estimate]). Create communication standards for overseas suppliers.
PC bundle Internal use + B2B resale at volume pricing AI-driven memory shortage: DRAM spot ~3-5x above mid-2025, NAND ~2-4x [Counterpoint Research, TrendForce]. Samsung delayed DDR4 phase-out (extended into 2026). Micron exited Crucial consumer SSD/DRAM line (Feb 2026). ASML EUV lithography monopoly (100% market share) creates upstream single-point-of-failure. Legacy $655 bundle [student estimate] now significantly more expensive. Secure contract pricing for DRAM/NAND (~70% of spot). Diversify memory suppliers beyond Samsung (SK Hynix, remaining Micron capacity). Define compatibility testing process. Assess whether current spot prices still support viable B2B resale margins.
